Style header for **materials**, not buildings. Prepended by `tools/pipeline/concept.py --kind materials`.

A material texture is the opposite of a concept image in almost every way: no object, no
camera angle, no composition, no lighting. It is a flat piece of surface that has to sit next
to copies of itself without a visible seam, and it has to carry no light of its own, because
the light comes from the game.

Everything below the line is prompt text.
