# Granary — the small agricultural building, and the adjacency test case

Small building, 2x2 tiles, ≤1,500 triangles. Sits beside farms for the adjacency bonus, so it
must read as agricultural at a glance and never be mistaken for housing.

**Prompt history.** The first version produced a convincing barn with a *lit window*, which
reads as somewhere people live — the exact failure it was written to avoid — and dropped the
grain chute entirely. Negative instructions ("no windows") are weak; the model needs the
positive feature stated first and repeated. Hence the current wording.

Everything below the line is prompt text.

---
a small raised timber grain store, its most prominent feature a long wooden grain chute
angled steeply down from an upper hatch to ground level, the building lifted clear of the
ground on short mushroom-shaped stone staddle stones, walls of dry ochre planking silvered
with age, steep thatched roof, plain blank windowless walls, a single small hatch high on the
gable, stacked grain sacks and a wooden scoop beneath the overhang, unlit and unheated, a
storehouse and not a dwelling
