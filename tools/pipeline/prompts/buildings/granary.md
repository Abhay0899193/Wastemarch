# Granary — the small agricultural building, and the adjacency test case

Small building, 2x2 tiles, ≤1,500 triangles. Sits beside farms for the adjacency bonus, so it
must read as agricultural at a glance and never be mistaken for housing.

Everything below the line is prompt text.

---
a small raised timber granary on short stone staddle stones, walls of dry ochre planking
silvered with age, steep thatched roof, an external grain chute angled down one side, sacks
stacked under the overhang, a low door, no windows, unmistakably a store for grain rather than
a place anyone sleeps
