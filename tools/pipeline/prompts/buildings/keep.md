# Keep — the player's first building and the game's anchor

Large building, 4x4 tiles, ≤4,000 triangles. Chapter 1 opens with it broken; by Chapter 6 it
is the tallest thing the player owns. It has to read as *repairable*, not as a ruin.

Everything below the line is prompt text.

---
a squat stone keep with a single square tower, thick weathered walls of pale bone-grey stone,
one corner visibly rebuilt with newer mismatched blocks, low pitched slate roof, narrow arrow
slits, a heavy timber door standing open with warm firelight spilling out, a crimson banner
hanging from the tower, small and worn, the whole building solid and defensible and clearly
lived in again after being abandoned
