# Watchtower — the silhouette stress test

Small footprint, 2x2 tiles, but tall. This is the hardest asset for the 64-pixel silhouette
rule: almost all of its identity is in its outline, so if the style holds here it holds
anywhere.

Everything below the line is prompt text.

---
a tall narrow timber and stone watchtower on a square base, open railed platform at the top
under a small conical shingle roof, an exposed ladder running up one side, a brazier burning
on the platform casting warm light downward, a crimson pennant at the peak, weathered and
functional, tall and thin against the sky
